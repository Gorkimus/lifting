Antagonist Supersets - Hyper Pro & Vest Optimization - Zero Axial Spine Sparing
v3.4 Biomechanical Refinement & Dynamic Log Release: Replaced Pallof press with Hanging Ring/Bar Knee Tucks; upgraded Sit-ups to Spine-Safe Decline Thoracic Crunches; added Bench-Braced Lateral Raises, Hammer Curls, Chest-Supported Y-Raises, and reordered Suitcase Carries to the finisher; converted workout logger to a dynamically synced exercise dropdown.
Coach's Protocol & Core Integrity
  • Daily Warm-Up: McGill Curl-Up (2x5 w/ 10s hold), Bird-Dog (2x8 / side), and Thoracic Rotations (2x8 / side) prior to every session.
  • Spinal Neutrality & Anti-Movement: Zero dynamic lumbar flexion/rotation under axial load. Core training targets 360-degree bracing and decompression.
  • The Superset Rule: Primary compound lifts are standalone to preserve CNS output. Accessory pairings (B1/B2, C1/C2) use antagonist supersets to maximize training density.
  • Progressive Overload: When maxing dumbbells at 45 lbs, overload via 3-second eccentrics, 1.5 reps, weighted vests, and lengthened partials.
  • Stop standalone compounds 1-2 RIR prior to muscular failure. Isolation accessory work is taken to 0-1 RIR.

Off-Day: Hip IR & Low Back Relief

Capsule Mobility & Control
Hip IR Protocol: If you cannot lift your ankle during RAILs, lean your torso slightly forward to open space. Maximal intent to lift stimulates new active range of motion!
Capsule Mobilization & Neuromuscular Control
1. Prone Hip IR Windshield Wipers Capsule Mobilization
2 Sets x 10-12 Reps (3s pause at bottom)
Lie on stomach, knees at 90 degrees wider than hips. Let feet fall outward away from each other. Pins pelvis flat to prevent lumbar twist.
2. 90/90 Hip IR PAILs / RAILs End-Range Isometrics
2 Rounds / side (60s stretch + 10s PAILs + 10s RAILs)
Focus on trail leg. Hold 60s stretch. PAILs: Drive rear ankle/shin down into floor for 10s. RAILs: Try to hover/pull rear ankle up off floor for 10s with outer hip muscles.
3. Seated Block Squeeze & Outward Ankle Hover Active IR Strength
2 Sets x 8-10 Reps / side (3s hold at peak)
Sit tall on bench, squeeze a foam block between knees. Actively swing one ankle outward without hiking pelvis.
4. Posterior-Tilted Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch Anterior Chain Length
2 Sets x 45-60 Seconds / side
Squeeze rear glute hard and tuck pelvis underneath. Shift forward only 1-2 inches. Keeps lower back flat.
5. Side-Lying Active IR Leg Lifts Lateral Pelvic Stability
2 Sets x 12 Reps / side (2s hold at top)
Lie on side, rotate top leg inward with toes pointing toward floor. Lift leg up and slightly back. Fires glute medius in internal rotation.
6. Bird-Dog Dynamic Bracing 360 Core / Erectors
2 Sets x 6 Reps / side (3s hold at peak)
Drive heel straight back and reach arm forward. Lock neutral spine with zero lumbar hyperextension.
